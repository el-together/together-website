FAQ

Questions, answered once.

Is Together free?

Yes. Everything essential is free, with unlimited projects and members. A Premium plan adds more Chip and new tools.

How many people can join a project?

As many as you need. Projects and members are unlimited on the free plan.

Does everyone need the app?

Yes. Each person opens your invite link on their phone, gets the app and lands in your project. It takes a minute.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Chip is there when you want it. Together works just as well without it.

Can I use it just for myself?

Yes. Keep your own week, to-do's and notes in the same place as your group plans.

Who can see my project?

Only the people you invite. Nothing is public, and nobody joins without a link from you.

I got an invite. What do I do?

Open the link on your phone and get the app. Your group is waiting inside.

I'm organizing a course or an event. Can you help?

Yes. Book a free 30-minute setup call and we'll help you get it ready for your group.
