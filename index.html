Made for

The problem

The information is there. The clarity isn't.

Every group starts the same way: a chat, a few links, a lot of good will. Then this happens.

Sunday night, twenty people

Twenty people in the same class. Sunday night the group chat still asks: what was due for tomorrow?

Four or five things in your head

For this week and the next. They stay there, a knot, until you write them down somewhere the group can see.

Held in one head

One person holding the whole plan for everyone. Every question goes to them.

How it works

One project. Everyone on the same page.

  1. Start a project for your group

    It holds the plan for everyone: the dates, the lists, the costs and the chat.

  2. Everyone adds what they know

    Write it down once, where the whole group can see it.

  3. Everyone sees what is next

    Open the project and the next step is there. Nobody has to ask.
