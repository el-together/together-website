The problem

The information is there. The clarity isn't.

Every group starts the same way: a chat, a few links, a lot of good will. Then this happens.

Sunday night, twenty people

Everyone in the class got the same lesson.

On Sunday night the group chat still asks: what was due for tomorrow?

Four or five things in your head

They stay there, a knot, until you write them down somewhere the group can see.

Held in one head

Every question goes to them. Nobody else can see what is next.
