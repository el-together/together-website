Clarity

We use the word clarity a lot. In our calls, in our notes, in the way we talk about what we build. It can sound big and a little vague, so we want to say exactly what we mean by it.

Clarity is knowing the next step. The what, not the how.

It is not the whole plan, and it is not a method. It is what comes next. How you get there is up to you, and to the people you are doing it with.

Most of the time, the information isn't missing. There is too much noise around it. In a group, what you need to know has often been said already. It is in a thread, in a link, in a message from last week. Adding more doesn't help. Every new message is one more thing to read past. The information is there. It just isn't clear.

We like to think of clarity as a small piece of seeing the future. Not next year. Not even next week. The next three hours, with certainty. You know where you need to be, and so do the others. That is the kind of clarity everyone wants.

What does that give you? You can let go. You don't have to stay on alert in case you forget something. You stop going over the plan in your head at night. You get to be more present. Think of a kid at a ping-pong table. They are not thinking about the score, or about what comes after the game. They only look at the next ball.

There is a scene we keep coming back to. Twenty people in the same class, with the same lessons. On Sunday night, someone asks in the group chat what was due for tomorrow. Then someone else asks the same thing. Nobody did anything wrong. The information was there. It had been shared, maybe more than once. It just wasn't clear.

Progress doesn't have to be stressful.

Clarity is not a push to do more. Be as productive as you like. Some weeks that is a lot, and some weeks it is very little. Both are fine. We don't think anyone needs one more reason to feel behind. Knowing what is next can make either kind of week feel lighter.

Our promise is a small one, and that is on purpose. We want to create that shared clarity, and to anticipate it, so the answer is already there before anyone has to ask on a Sunday night. Nothing bigger than that. Just the next step, clear for the whole group. The how stays yours.
